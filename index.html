Help & Supermarket Software Guide

How do I generate an automated bill?

Add desired products to your cart from the Browse Products section, click Proceed to Checkout, and press Generate Official Supermarket Bill. You can print or download the bill directly.

What is the applicable tax rate?

As per Indian supermarket retail guidelines for packed groceries, a standard 5% GST is dynamically calculated on the cart subtotal.

Which promo code works for testing?

You can use promo code SUPER10 for a 10% discount on your order!
